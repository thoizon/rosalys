Privacy Policy – Rosalys

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Rosalys (Android, package com.thoizon.rosalys) uses, why, and what choices you have. Rosalys is published by Thoizon, an independent developer (an individual, not a company), referred to below as “I” or “the developer”.

1. In short

  • Rosalys has no account system of its own and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • I do not run any server: the developer receives none of your personal data.
  • Your progress is stored on your phone and, if you choose, in your Google Play Games account.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for your consent first.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play; I never see your payment details.
  • No data is sold.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, flowers, “dew” currency, bonuses, “My island” items), your settings (language, music, sounds), whether “No ads” was purchased, the identifiers of purchases already credited, and a counter used to space out ads. This data stays on your device and is not sent to the developer. It is deleted when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the game.

3. Google Play Games (cloud save)

When Google Play Games is available, the game signs in automatically with your Play Games profile so that your progress can be saved to your Google account and restored on another phone. The saved file contains only game progress and settings as described above. It is stored by Google, under Google’s Privacy Policy. You can sign out or manage it from Settings › Google save in the game, and you can delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games settings on your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rosalys is free and funded by ads: optional rewarded videos (you choose to watch them for a bonus) and an interstitial ad every few levels (never if you bought “No ads”). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • your IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and ad interactions (views, clicks);
  • diagnostic data such as crashes of the ad component.

Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game displays Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes. If you refuse, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy and ads button in the game’s settings.

Everywhere. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads), which stops ad personalisation based on it.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases (“No ads”, dew packs, gardener pack) are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled only by Google; the developer never receives them. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity Technologies) to talk to Google Play and checks Google’s signature on each receipt on your device. Unity may process technical data (such as an installation identifier and device information) to provide this service, under Unity’s Privacy Policy.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

Personalised advertising and the related use of your advertising ID rely on your consent. Saving your progress, cloud saves you enable, and processing purchases are needed to provide the game you asked for. Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention rely on the legitimate interest of funding a free game.

7. Children

Rosalys is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child’s data has been processed, contact me and I will help have it removed.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device data is kept until you clear the app data or uninstall. Cloud saves remain in your Google account until you delete them. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google’s policies. Since the developer holds no personal data, there is nothing to delete on my side, but I can guide you through the steps above.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the LGPD in Brazil), you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. For data held by Google, use your Google Account tools. You can also contact me, and you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Rosalys मुफ़्त है और विज्ञापनों से चलता है: वैकल्पिक इनाम वाले वीडियो (बोनस पाने के लिए आप ख़ुद देखना चुनते हैं) और हर कुछ लेवल के बाद एक इंटरस्टीशियल विज्ञापन (अगर आपने “विज्ञापन-मुक्त” ख़रीदा है तो कभी नहीं)। विज्ञापन Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) देता है। विज्ञापन दिखाने और मापने के लिए AdMob ये जानकारी इकट्ठा और प्रोसेस कर सकता है:

सहमति। EEA, यूके और स्विट्ज़रलैंड में, कोई भी विज्ञापन माँगने से पहले गेम Google का सहमति फ़ॉर्म (User Messaging Platform) दिखाता है। आप स्वीकार, अस्वीकार या उद्देश्य चुन सकते हैं। अस्वीकार करने पर सिर्फ़ गैर-व्यक्तिगत या सीमित विज्ञापन दिखाए जाते हैं। गेम की सेटिंग्स में गोपनीयता और विज्ञापन बटन से आप कभी भी अपना फ़ैसला बदल सकते हैं।

हर जगह। Android सेटिंग्स (सेटिंग्स › गोपनीयता › विज्ञापन, या Google › विज्ञापन) में आप अपनी विज्ञापन ID रीसेट या डिलीट कर सकते हैं, जिससे उस पर आधारित विज्ञापन निजीकरण बंद हो जाता है।

और जानें: Google अपनी सेवाओं का इस्तेमाल करने वाले ऐप्स की जानकारी कैसे इस्तेमाल करता है।
